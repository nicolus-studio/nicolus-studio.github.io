Primate Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short

  • Primate Rush does not ask for an account, a name or an email address.
  • Nicolus Studio has no server: we receive no data about you or your games.
  • Your settings stay on your phone.
  • The game is free and funded by an ad shown at launch, served by Google AdMob. Google collects the data needed for this ad, with your consent where the law requires it.

Who we are

Primate Rush is published by Nicolus Studio: Nicolas Sallier, sole trader (France), SIRET 132 621 087 00015, [BUSINESS ADDRESS]. For any question about your data: nicolus.studio@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

To keep your settings from one launch to the next, the game stores on your phone, and only on your phone:

  • the language you chose;
  • the graphics quality setting.

Your score and best score are only kept while the game is open.

This information is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. If Android backup is turned on, it may be included in your Google account backup, which Google manages; we have no access to it.

Sharing your score

When you tap "Share", the game creates a picture of your score and opens the Android share menu. You choose the app and the recipient yourself. Nothing goes through Nicolus Studio.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

At launch, the game may show an ad served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and fight fraud, Google may collect directly from your device:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • technical information about the device and system (model, Android version, language);
  • your interactions with ads (views, clicks);
  • diagnostic data (crashes, performance).

Google processes this data under its own rules: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may process this data outside the European Union, with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form provided by Google appears the first time you launch the game. There you choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads.

  • If you accept, Google may tailor ads to your interests.
  • If you decline, Google may show non-personalised ads, which use a minimum of data (for example to limit how often the same ad repeats, or to detect fraud).

You can change your mind at any time with the "Privacy" option in the game menu. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising ID in Android settings (Google, then Ads).

Personalised advertising is based on your consent. Non-personalised advertising is based on Nicolus Studio's legitimate interest in funding a free game.

Permissions

Primate Rush only uses Internet access, to load ads. It does not access your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera.

Retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own rules.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. As Nicolus Studio holds no data about you, requests about advertising data should be made to Google, for example from your Google Account. For any question, email us at nicolus.studio@gmail.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
