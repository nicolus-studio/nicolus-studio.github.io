Primate Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Who we are

Primate Rush is published by Nicolus Studio, France (see the legal notice). Contact: nicolus.studio@gmail.com.

What the game stores

The game does not ask for an account, a name or an email address, and sends us no data. It only stores, on your phone, the language you chose and the graphics quality setting, which are deleted when you uninstall the game. The "Share" button opens the Android share menu: nothing goes through us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows an ad at launch, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and fight fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), technical information about the device, your interactions with ads and diagnostic data. Google processes and keeps this data under its own rules, possibly outside the European Union with the safeguards required by the GDPR: see how Google uses this information.

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google form asks you at first launch whether you accept personalised ads; they are then based on your consent. If you decline, non-personalised ads may be shown; they are based on our legitimate interest in funding a free game. You can change your mind with the "Privacy" option in the game menu, and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability provided by the GDPR. We hold no data about you: for advertising data, contact Google from your Google Account. For any question: nicolus.studio@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
